Simple & Safe

How MirrorLock Works

Keep an eye on long-running AI pipelines without worrying about pets, kids, or accidental keystrokes.

1

Lock with one click

Click the menu bar icon or press ⌘⇧M. MirrorLock immediately blocks all keyboard, mouse, and trackpad inputs.

2

Watch your AI work

Your screen remains completely visible behind a subtle translucent tint. Your Mac will never sleep, allowing Claude or Cursor to finish undisturbed.

3

Unlock with your wrist

Double-press your Apple Watch side button, tap your Android Watch, or rest your finger on Touch ID to unlock instantly.

Capabilities

Everything You Need

Engineered natively in Swift for peak macOS performance, battery efficiency, and privacy.

Live Screen Mirror

Uses ScreenCaptureKit so your terminals, IDEs, and browsers stay 100% visible while you step away from your desk.

Complete Input Lock

Hard-blocks keyboard keys, mouse clicks, mouse movements, scrolling, gestures, and media keys at the system level.

Watch & Touch ID Unlock

Unlock effortlessly with Apple Watch, Wear OS Android Watch, or Mac Touch ID without typing passwords.

Keeps Your Mac Awake

Maintains an IOKit power assertion while locked, guaranteeing that your display and CPU will not sleep mid-task.

Auto-Lock When Idle

Optionally locks after 1–30 minutes of inactivity, with a handy 10-second cancelable countdown HUD.

Intruder Warning & Eye

Accidental or intentional keystrokes trigger an eye surveillance icon, and touching the trackpad flashes a red border alert.

Multi-Monitor Support

Mirrors your primary screen while cleanly blanking secondary displays in black. Unplugging displays never unlocks your Mac.

100% Private & Local

Zero network requests, zero telemetry, zero analytics. Everything runs locally on your machine under an MIT license.

Emergency Exit Key

Includes an optional customizable emergency escape shortcut (e.g. ⌃⌥⌘Esc) so you can never get locked out.

Convenience

Unlock Your Way

No need to sit down and type a password just to check on your code.

Apple Watch

Apple Watch Unlock

Double-press the side button on your Apple Watch or use the optional "Tap to unlock" feature to dismiss the lock screen instantly using Apple's local authentication.

Wear OS

Android Watch Unlock

Built-in framework for Android Wear OS smartwatches. Send a tap-to-unlock signal from your watch to MirrorLock via your local encrypted connection.

Touch ID

Touch ID Sensor

Rest your finger on your Mac keyboard's Touch ID sensor. Validated instantaneously through Apple's Secure Enclave hardware.

Get Started

Install MirrorLock

Choose your preferred installation method below.

Install cleanly via Homebrew cask in a single command:

brew install --cask shubhransh-gupta/tap/mirrorlock

Installs a signed .pkg directly into /Applications with zero Gatekeeper warnings.

Or run the automated install script directly in your terminal:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/shubhransh-gupta/mirrorLock/main/scripts/install.sh | bash

Downloads the latest release and places MirrorLock in your Applications folder automatically.
